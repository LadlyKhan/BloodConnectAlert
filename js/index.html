Blood request coordination

Connecting urgent blood needs with registered donors

Submit a blood request, get it verified, and alert nearby donors in stages. Blood banks keep reported stock up to date so hospitals can find it.

How it works

Every request is checked before any donor is contacted.

  1. 1

    Request submitted

    A requester enters the blood group, hospital, number of bags, urgency and the date and time needed.

  2. 2

    Verifier review

    A verifier approves or rejects it. A rejection always comes with a reason shown to the requester.

  3. 3

    Staged donor alerts

    Alerts start within 3 km, then widen to 7 km, 15 km and the whole district. No donor gets the same alert twice.

  4. 4

    Facility decides

    Donors who accept go to the facility. Only authorised staff record screening results and whether a unit is usable.

Who it is for

Choose the account that matches what you do. Each account has one role.

Donor

Set your availability and area, and respond to alerts that match your blood group.

Requester

Submit a blood request and follow its status from review to fulfilment.

Hospital

Search reported stock at blood banks and send inquiries. Starts as pending verification.

Blood Bank

Record donations, track units and stock, and answer hospital inquiries. Starts as pending verification.

Verifier and Admin accounts are created by an Admin and cannot be registered here.

What this demonstration does not do

  • It does not make medical or compatibility decisions.
  • Reported availability is not a reservation.
  • SMS, email and push messages are simulated.
  • It connects to no real hospital or blood bank system.
  • It handles whole blood only, not components.
  • All people, hospitals and quantities are sample data.
